About the artist

Painting a softer way of seeing.

I am Ajna, a Mauritius-based watercolor artist. My work begins with observation: the shifting light over the ocean, a vase of garden flowers, the tender geometry of a familiar street.

With loose washes and layered colour, I create paintings that invite a little more calm into the everyday.
