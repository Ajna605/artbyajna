About the artist

Capturing island life in watercolour

Watercolour paintings inspired by the everyday life in Mauritius.

From the studio

I am a born and bred Mauritian artist. I like to capture Mauritius in all its different lights and angles: simple, beautiful, shabby, vibrant, ghetto, all the good things.

There is too much that goes unnoticed in our everyday life: the corner shop on our street, an old stone church, a kitchen boasting the oldest plastic container. Things you may only know as being Mauritian.

So I paint what I find inspiring, from the most mundane to the grandest our island offers. Each work is an invitation to pause and notice what is already there.
